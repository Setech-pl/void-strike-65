## 2. Formations, entries and paths: present versus M3

**Present code provides two different compositions.** A Heavy wave requests successive formations of up to two same-archetype Heavies, with an optional one-Light companion. A Light-dominant wave requests successive members of one Light archetype. The compiler refuses a second member on a Light-dominant wave, so there is no authored mixed Wingman/Interceptor Light formation. Count is the number requested over time, not the simultaneous population. (`scripts/level-compiler.mjs:719–748`; `src/c/lifecycle.c:908–945,1382–1412`.)

For standalone Lights, entry X 48–200 and spacing are authorable; all members of a wave receive the same entry. The Director enforces minimum spacing 16 for Light and 24 for Heavy waves. Successful Light admissions decrement the remaining count; blocked admissions retry, and the wave lock persists until its members leave. Thus spacing is a minimum opportunity interval, not a guarantee of a geometrical formation. Wingman gives a descending column; Interceptor gives a descending, player-seeking stream. Wingman escorts instead follow their Heavy leader’s movement. (`scripts/level-compiler.mjs:778–781`; `src/c/director.c:189–198,283–339`; `src/c/lifecycle.c:908–945,1091–1146`.)

**Present data already demonstrates several Lights, in level 2.** Its first swarm sector requests six Interceptors at X 88, six Wingmen at X 160, then six hunter Interceptors at X 124, spacing 24. Its post-capital swarm sector requests three eight-Interceptor waves, spacing 20, entries 64/184/96 with hunter/lancer skins. Both sectors request Light cap 3 and Heavy cap 0. Elite sectors have Wingman escorts, including Bomber + Wingman, and sector 6 includes a standalone four-member lancer Interceptor wave, but elite cap 1 serializes its live population. (`assets/levels/level-02.json:194–285,301–340,399–470`.)

There is **no current authored path evaluator or formation library**. The compiler rejects non-default `path`, emits `wave_path = $FF`, and leaves the already-reserved 96-byte path payload unused. `mirror` and `afterCleared` are accepted/packed flags but are not consumed by the current Director/lifecycle wave execution; they do not create mirrored or clearance-controlled waves today. (`scripts/level-compiler.mjs:82–86,791–801,894–905`; `src/c/director.c:283–339`; `assets/levels/level-02.json:64–70`; `docs/level-authoring.md:121–140`.)

M3 states:

> “A sine, an arc, a snake and a loop are still four-segment envelopes.”

— `docs/plans/m3-waves-heavy.md:524–525`, plan recorded by `5fd528e`, 2026-10-02.

> “every path a level uses is in that level's payload block (`path[8]`, 96 B, already in every image), and the standard library is a table of named paths in the compiler.”

— `docs/plans/m3-waves-heavy.md:534–537`, owner decision recorded by `e3bd098`, 2026-10-02.

These are piecewise envelopes, not a mathematical sine renderer: four 3-byte segments specify signed vertical speed, duration, cell-sized lateral movement/period, pursuit, segment looping, fire permission and retirement. M3 would add named-path compilation/validation, Director path/mirror publication, two state bytes per Light and the C evaluator. Every second member can mirror lateral movement. Segment-held fire, a shared-pulse volley and column-conditional fire are also planned. Heavy paths are excluded from M3 V1; M3-H separately handles Heavy profiles/holds. (`docs/plans/m3-waves-heavy.md:511–563,582–597,619–637`.)

The planned smoke covers level-2 snake, mirrored sine, upward dive, volley and held fire; the plan explicitly says **“level 1's data untouched.”** These behaviours are planned/prototyped, not implemented production features. (`docs/plans/m3-waves-heavy.md:827–831,1155–1170`.)

“Elite” is the space-sector subtype that permits Heavy formations and caps accompanying/standalone Lights at one. It does not imply an elite Light archetype or the proposed armoured Raider. Space defaults to swarm if subtype is omitted. Capital forbids ordinary enemies; swarm forbids Heavy archetypes. Boss authoring allows at most one escort in the permitted opening region, and none in region 1; level 1 authors none. (`scripts/level-compiler.mjs:618–623,649–671`; `docs/level-authoring.md:197–200`.)

The post-capital wave is **Raider + Wingman in an elite sector**, with authored Light cap 1. It is not a standalone Light wave, and “single” means at most one live companion, not one Light for the whole six-count wave. A Heavy wave’s count requests formations; companions can be admitted for successive formations. Sector 3 can end before every requested formation is delivered, because world-row progression is not an enemy-clear barrier. (`assets/levels/level-01.json:168–199`; `src/c/lifecycle.c:1382–1412`; `src/c/director.c:443–529`.)

Raising that JSON cap to 3 would still yield effective cap 1. Swarm sectors permit several Lights, but cannot author Raider/Bomber waves. Those are the relevant constraints on a data-only demonstration.
