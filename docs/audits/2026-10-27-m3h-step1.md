## Confirmed conflicts requiring a plan adjustment

1. **One flag bit has two consumers.** Plan §3.2 allocates bit 6 to armoured
   Raiders. Current `scripts/level-compiler.mjs` encodes Interceptor `loops`
   in bits 6–7, also on Heavy waves with an Interceptor escort;
   `light_admit` copies the whole flags byte and the Light controller consumes
   those bits. Both an assault Heavy with one escort loop and an armoured
   Heavy with no loops would encode as `$48`. The shipped level-1 sector-4
   Heavy has two escort loops (`$88`); setting the planned armoured bit would
   change that to three (`$C8`). Keeping the precise plan encoding would
   change existing authored gameplay. Bit 5 is currently unused, but adopting
   it changes the specified format and needs the decision below.
2. **H2's planned home moved.** Plan §5 puts its 96-byte armoured executor
   in the Light kernel's window record 9. L1 moved the complete kernel to
   `$3643–$3AFF`: **1,196/1,213 B, 17 free**, a **79-byte budget shortfall**.
   The stated fallback in `PICKUP_CODE` has **7 free B**, rather than 65:
   **89 B short** on its own. Even combining those 7 B with the primary pin's
   45 B left after the planned 58-byte H1 leaves **44 B short**. These are
   shortfalls against the plan's budget, not sizes of newly compiled code.
   Moving H2 to the existing Director window record 8 can fit the aggregate
   budget, but is a different linking/publication route from both specified
   routes. No twelfth DFMC record is available.
3. **Payload follow-up accounting changed.** M5's summary takes 10 of the
   former 32 `hull_params` bytes. There are **22**, so session 2's 17-byte
   `heavyLook` leaves **5**, not 15. Its immediate allocation still fits;
   preserving the old 15-byte remainder would require another 10 bytes.
   This does not independently block session 1.

### Bit-5 search before production edits

The required search covered `scripts/level-compiler.mjs`, Director/lifecycle
C and headers, `src/main.s`, and all `src/hybrid` ASM. Raw results are in
`build/evidence/m3h-step1/implementation/bit5-audit.log`.

- Compiler `:810–815`: the comment says “bits 4–5 trigger mode”, but the
  expression writes only bit 4 for `afterCleared`; bits 6–7 encode loops.
- `src/c/director.c:103–105,495`: `WAVE_FLAG_AFTER_CLEARED` is `$10`; no
  `$20` WaveDef mask. `$20` `FLAG_BOSS_DUE` is a different state byte.
- `src/c/lifecycle.c:122,963,1180,1199`: escort admission copies flags
  unchanged; loop policy masks `$C0` and subtracts `$40`. Bit 5 is ignored.
- ASM references to `heavy_wave_flags` import/export the C symbol; no
  bit-5 reader/writer was found. Other `$20` literals refer to unrelated
  IRQ, projectile, palette, glyph or screen operations.

Thus bit 5 is available, and the approved allocation is now recorded in
`docs/level-authoring.md` and `docs/memory-map.md`, explicitly **not installed**
in production after rollback. No new WaveDef field, loop re-encoding or
level-1 flag change was made. Level-1 active flags remain
