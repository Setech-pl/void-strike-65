Mount an ATR as D1. Choose EASY, NORMAL or HARD in the menu; start with NORMAL.

- `pilot.atr`: level2 sector4, teach → mirrored cross → tracking pressure →
  quiet upward release. Four groups of8; up to4 live Lights.
- `level-2.atr`: full level2, including the three-group opening and Heavy sectors.
- `volley.atr`: diagnostic four-Light wide-hold/volley then tracking/combined.
- `approach.atr`: level2 Bombers before capital; check departure and clean handoff.
- `boss.atr`: region1 boss for escorts, pause/death/restart and guarded phase checks.
- `campaign.atr`: normal level1; familiar baseline, capital and boss progression.

What to look for:

1. Play `pilot` without fire once, then kill groups quickly. All32 members and
   the final quiet release should appear; the next group follows as the field
   clears. Entry has a short cue (up to about0.7s for the hidden mirrored fan).
2. Watch left, right and centre: broad sweeps, opposite-side mirrored arrivals
   and crossings. Look for usable gaps; report any unavoidable crossfire.
3. Quiet approaches/exits stay quiet. Column fire stays near your column;
   volleys use shared beats with no delayed extras. Try EASY and HARD too.
4. Hunter takes2 hits, lancer4; other looks inherit archetype HP. Weapons and
   points remain familiar. Check hit flash and clean top/bottom/side erase.
5. Bombers stop firing and exit sideways; Heavy clears before capital. Try
   pause, death, restart and boss escorts; no corridor strips or stale fighters.
