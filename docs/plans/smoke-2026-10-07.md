### 1.2 The fix

The in-band shot cells are written after the band has been shown.
SECTOR_COMPLETION, late in every frame, now enters `boss_shots_late`
(slot E): it waits for the band's last line (VCOUNT 44; never inside a frame
that overran into the next, VCOUNT < 12), restores last frame's cells (each

it, then jumps to `boss_completion`. UPDATE keeps the meeting
(`boss_shot_meet`, without the drawing). The band then shows every frame's
shots on every row, a frame later - as the playfield below shows them
(`publish_fighter_projectile_overlays`), so a shot crossing from the ring into
the band no longer jumps.
