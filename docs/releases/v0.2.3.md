## Title

Void Strike 65 v0.2.3 — boots on real hardware

## Body

Checkpoint pre-release on the way to v0.3.0 (milestone M5: loading platform and bosses). **This is the first ATR that boots on a real Atari.**

## Fixed: the disk now loads on real hardware

Every earlier ATR (v0.1.0 to v0.2.2) stopped on a red screen after about 15 seconds on a real Atari 65XE/800XL. The loader asked the operating system for most of the game's sectors without the data. Emulators with an accelerated disk routine (Atari800's default "SIO patch") loaded them anyway, so the fault never showed there. v0.2.3 loads through the standard disk protocol, as the real machine does, and is tested that way. Loading takes longer at standard SIO speed: about 30 seconds from power-on to the main menu on an SIO2SD (27 s measured in an emulator without the SIO patch).

## What's new since v0.2.2

- **Light enemies in level 1 after the capital ship:** a swarm of both Light types, two elite Raider sectors and a Bomber pair between them.
- **Sector flow.** A space sector ends as soon as its waves are spent and the field is clear, instead of running out its full length; much less empty flying after the capital ship. The boss arrives sooner, and it waits until falling debris has left the screen.
- **The boss platform** for the coming regions: the booster bar stays on screen through the boss's arrival, and no gun fires on the frame you destroy a module. The boss finale (three-shot volleys from every surviving gun once the last armour falls) exists only on the review routes for regions 2–4; level 1's Blockade Breaker fights as before.
- Your shots stay visible in the band under the boss until they reach their target. The radio-chatter line no longer overlaps the statistics on the summary screen.
- The target machine is the 64 KB Atari 65XE / 800XL (PAL); a 130XE also works.

## Known issues

- Loading at standard SIO speed is slow (about 30 s to the menu); a fast loader is planned as an optional later step.
- Difficulty is not tuned yet (balancing is planned for a later milestone).

## How to play

Download `void-strike-65.atr` and boot it as drive 1 on a PAL Atari 65XE/800XL (64 KB), with or without OPTION held, from an SIO2SD or a disk drive, or in an emulator, e.g. `atari800 -xl -pal -nopatch void-strike-65.atr` (`-nopatch` loads like the real machine; without it, it loads faster). The game saves your best score to the disk, so play a copy.
