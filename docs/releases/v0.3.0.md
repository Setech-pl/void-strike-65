## Title

Void Strike 65 v0.3.0 — four bosses

## Body

Pre-release at the end of milestone M5 (loading platform and bosses). The
campaign's four regions each have their own boss now; the downloadable ATR
plays level 1 and its boss, and the bosses of regions 2-4 are reachable on
review builds until their levels are authored.

## New: four bosses, each with its own mechanic

- **Blockade Breaker** (levels 1-3): two laser cannons take turns under an
  armoured spine, behind a belt of armour plates.
- **Bastion** (levels 4-6): a round hangar with its reactor core between two
  laser cannons in armoured bays, and a gun rail on each flank. Destroy a
  rail gun and the rail slides inward, opening that side's cannon's armour;
  slide it twice and it covers the cannon, which can no longer be hurt and
  keeps firing. The hangar gate stays locked until every cannon and rail gun
  is gone or covered. Open a cannon's armour, destroy it before the rail
  closes in.
- **Siege Spine** (levels 7-9): a force field in two halves, each falling
  with its generator.
- **Void Citadel** (levels 10-12): a still fortress whose gravity well pulls
  you towards its core's laser.

Every boss: a WARNING screen with its name while it loads, modules that flash
when hit, a laser that warns before it fires, an escort of Light laser
fighters from your first weapon kill, final salvos once its armour is gone
(regions 2-4), and the level summary after it.

## Also new since v0.2.3

- The Light laser fighter takes three hits, holds above you and fires instead
  of ramming, and can be shot in every phase of its flight.
- Your shots keep their colour right under every boss.

## Known issues

- Only level 1 ends on a boss in the downloadable ATR; levels 4-12 are not
  authored yet.
- Difficulty is tuned with a bot; real players' tuning is planned for a later
  milestone.
- Loading at standard SIO speed is slow (about 30 s to the menu).

## How to play

Download `void-strike-65.atr` and boot it as drive 1 on a PAL Atari
65XE/800XL (64 KB), from an SIO2SD or a disk drive, or in an emulator, e.g.
`atari800 -xl -pal -nopatch void-strike-65.atr`. The game saves your best
score to the disk, so play a copy.
